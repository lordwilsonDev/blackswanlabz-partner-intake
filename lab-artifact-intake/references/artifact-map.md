## Archived pages (stubs)
`master-sop.md`, `operator-sop.md` and `north-star-architecture.md` are one-paragraph stubs: the lab held them back to stay inside its size budget and keeps the full text in its own git history (the stub names the commit). `fetch_lab.py` follows that pointer and saves the full text under the normal name, with the stub beside it as `<name>.stub.md`; the manifest says `git-history@<commit>`. If it cannot recover one, it prints `STUB:`: tell the person that page is not available and do not summarize the stub as if it were the document.
