# The five questions (ask exactly these, in this order)

Use the host's multiple-choice question tool if it has one (allow several answers where marked "check all"). If not, print each question with lettered boxes `[ ]` and wait for the reply. Always allow "Not sure" and "Other (say it in a few words)". Never skip a question, never add a sixth, never answer for the person. Say why each one matters in one short line.

**Q1. Who is this for?** (check all)  Why: it decides which guardrails and which growth measures apply.
- A. People with disabilities or special needs (supported employment, job coaching, a workshop program)
- B. New hires
- C. Current employees
- D. Apprentices, students or interns
- E. Program participants or volunteers
- F. Just me or my small team
- G. Not sure

**Q2. What kind of place is it?** (check one)  Why: size decides how much to build (one page for a tiny shop, a full manual for a big one).
- A. One person or a tiny shop
- B. Small business, 2 to 15 people
- C. Growing business, 16 to 100 people
- D. Larger organization, 100+ people
- E. Nonprofit, co-packer or workshop that employs or trains people
- F. School, training provider or workforce partner
- G. Not started yet

**Q3. What do you need from the lab?** (check all)  Why: it picks which lab artifacts get fetched and drafted.
- A. Curriculum or training plan
- B. Skills your AI can use
- C. SOPs (standard operating procedures)
- D. Human-resources pack (roles, onboarding, support, review)
- E. KPIs and OKRs
- F. Grant-readiness package
- G. Show me everything the lab has

**Q4. How should growth be measured?** (check all)  Why: measures must match what the person can honestly show.
- A. Skills the person can demonstrate (competencies)
- B. How much support is needed over time (independence)
- C. Goals the person sets for themselves
- D. Attendance and retention
- E. Hours and wages
- F. Quality and safety
- G. Results for the employer (productivity)
- H. Not sure

**Q5. Funding and timing?** (check all that apply)  Why: a grant has hard eligibility gates; deadlines decide the order of work.
- A. Looking for a workforce, state or federal grant
- B. Looking for a foundation or private grant
- C. Already have a funder with reporting rules
- D. No grant: internal use only
- E. Deadline within 30 days
- F. Deadline within 3 months
- G. No deadline

## If an answer is missing or unclear
Ask that question once more in one short line. If it is still blank, record UNKNOWN and keep going: never answer for the person (found in the first test: Q5 was skipped). If the person describes something the boxes did not capture, such as daily learning from a plan schedule, add the matching need as INFERRED, tell them, and let them remove it.
